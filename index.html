Why This Event Exists

A Message From the Founder

When I was 15 years old, my grandmother had a stroke. For the next 15 years, I watched her slowly lose her ability to walk, her independence, and her dignity. It was heartbreaking to watch someone I loved endure so much pain and suffering, day after day, year after year.

Ten years after she passed away, the same tragedy struck my family again. When I was 25, my father began suffering from severe heart disease. I watched him go through multiple surgeries and years of pain and complications, until he finally passed away from heart disease five years ago.

Seeing both my grandmother and my father suffer needlessly broke something deep inside me.

After my dad's passing, I had a frightening wake-up call. I got tested and realized — I was next.

That moment shook me to my core.

Then I learned the devastating truth: 7 out of 10 people will spend the last 10 to 15 years of their lives in pain and suffering from chronic disease.

I've always believed with every fiber of my being that it doesn't have to be this way.

That kind of needless, prolonged suffering is not how life is supposed to be.

This realization became my calling.

This is exactly why I created the Optimal Health Summit.

Sid King
Founder, Optimal Health Summit
